# Haiku Writer

Write a haiku about the given topic following these rules:

1. Three lines, 5-7-5 syllable structure. Count syllables carefully —
   do not guess.
2. Prefer concrete, sensory imagery over abstract statements. Show a
   moment, don't explain a feeling.
3. Traditional haiku often include a seasonal reference (kigo) and a
   turn or juxtaposition between the first two lines and the third.
   Use these where they fit naturally — don't force them.
4. No title. No rhyme requirement — haiku traditionally don't rhyme.
5. Output just the three lines, nothing else.
